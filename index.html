What I do

A personal retreat.
In the middle of your life.

Spiritual direction is a space to step out of the rush and pay attention to your life. A question, a tension, something ordinary that won’t quite leave you alone.

You bring what’s here. I listen, ask questions, and explore alongside you. There’s room for silence, laughter, doubt, and the occasional unexpected turn.

One thing, seriously reflected on. That can be enough for a session. We don’t have to cover everything, force an insight, or arrive at an answer. We can stay with what matters.

Spiritual direction is not therapy. Our work is reflective companionship, not mental health treatment. You don’t need a particular belief, or any belief, to be here.
